## The Stablecoin — **USDrw**

**USDrw** is minted when users lock SPYx as collateral.  
It is designed for **stability, solvency, and long-term financial sovereignty**.

- **Collateral:** SPYx (tokenized S&P 500 ETF)
- **Solvency:** Reserves & minting verifiable on-chain
- **Resilience:** Lower volatility → significantly reduced liquidation likelihood

> **USDrw lets you stay in crypto while building real wealth.**
